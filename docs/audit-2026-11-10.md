# Audit of the current implementation (2026-11-10)

Scope: the design template (`template` branch of zohelo-bi, https://template.zohelo-bi.pages.dev), and how it sits
against the data platform (zohelo-data), the public site (zohelo) and the product principles
(`docs/product-principles.md`). Evidence was collected on 2026-11-10 from the repositories, GitHub and the built
template; numbers below are measured, not estimated.

## Verdict

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is the design and architecture worth keeping? | **Yes.** The foundations are sound and unusually well tested for a prototype. |
| Can it be shown to users now? | **Yes, as a labelled prototype to 5–10 chosen people for feedback.** Not as a product: every number is synthetic. |
| Can it go public as a product? | **No.** The template shows 288 indicators; the real catalogue publishes 7. That gap, not the UI, is what stands between today and a product. |
| Biggest risk | **Breadth.** The template keeps growing in features on sample data while the real data and the integration stand still. |

## What is strong

1. **One data interface.** Every page reads through `DataProvider` (`catalog`, `series`, `latest`); the sample and
   the live provider implement the same three calls. Moving to real data is a data-contract job, not a rewrite.
2. **Content as files.** Dashboards (`dashboards/domains/*.yaml`), official thresholds (`dashboards/yardsticks.yaml`)
   and chart looks are data, validated by `bun ops/check-dashboards.ts`. A domain expert can change a dashboard or a
   threshold without touching code.
3. **Rules instead of AI.** Guardrails (no summing of averages, no index of a rate), findings in titles, unusual
   moves, look suggestions and scorecard verdicts are deterministic and tested. This is the product's real
   differentiator and it matches the principles.
4. **Quality gates.** 892 unit tests, a 57-page UI suite (axe: 0 violations, focus, no sideways scroll, minimum text
   size), NVDA and VoiceOver runs in CI, Polish and English throughout. Few production BI tools hold this bar.
5. **Business-first features that BI tools lack.** Copy to Excel, editable PowerPoint, collections as
   board-or-slides, scorecards judged by official thresholds.

## What is weak or risky

| # | Finding | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sample data only; the real catalogue is far behind.** | Template: 288 indicators, 12 domains, 12 sources. `zohelo-data/warehouse/seeds/kpi.csv`: 21 KPIs, **7 published** (17 annual, 2 monthly, 2 daily). | Critical |
| 2 | **The live provider cannot feed the template yet.** | `data/live.ts`: `dimensions: []`, `geographies: ["PL"]`, `next_release: null`, `year_ago_value: null`. No breakdowns, no EU countries, no release calendar, no aggregation type per KPI, no thresholds source. | Critical |
| 3 | **The template has never been reviewed or merged.** | `template` is 137 commits and +73,064 lines ahead of `main`, with no pull request. `main` still carries the old portal; 20 open sprint issues and 5 open PRs in zohelo-bi refer to that old UI. | High |
| 4 | **The explorer shows controls, not the chart.** | At 1440×900 the chart starts at about y = 830 px: header, ask box, two rows of wells, title, finding, export row, look strip, note and suggestions come first. This contradicts "simplicity first". | High |
| 5 | **Scope against capacity.** | 48 chart types in the engine (31 in the picker, 93 variants), 55 looks, 35 explorer kinds, 13 dashboards, collections, four export formats, a rule-based ask box: about 39,000 lines of TypeScript and 8,800 of CSS for one owner. Each feature is cheap to add with agents and expensive to keep right on real data. | High |
| 6 | **Operations are unwatched while work is on the template.** | The NBP pipeline has failed at `silver_gold.build` since 2026-11-09 (two runs, no later success); the alert step failed too. `fx_rate` is one of the 7 published KPIs. Six alert issues are open; development on zohelo-data `main` stopped on 2026-11-03. | High |
| 7 | **`main` is unprotected in two repositories.** | zohelo and zohelo-bi: `protected=false`, no rulesets (zohelo-data is protected). Open since 2026-10-27 as owner actions. | Medium |
| 8 | **One 2 MB bundle.** | `index.js` 2,093 kB (661 kB gzipped), no route splitting; it includes the 156 kB sample catalogue and the explorer on every page. PowerPoint export is already loaded on demand (364 kB). | Medium |
| 9 | **Personal layer lives in the browser only.** | Favourites, saved views, collections and topics are in `localStorage`: lost on another device or after clearing data. Fine for a prototype, not for "your presentations". | Medium |
| 10 | **Dead weight.** | About 10,000 lines of the old portal remain in `apps/portal/src` outside `tpl/`; `vega`, `vega-lite` and `vega-embed` are still dependencies of `packages/visuals` though the template does not use them. | Low |
| 11 | **Own chart engine.** | `packages/visuals` is 12,300 lines for 48 chart types. It gives exact control (IBCS, scorecard, accessibility tables) but every new type is ours to maintain. Acceptable only if the type list is frozen. | Low |
| 12 | **Unverified Office behaviour.** | Excel paste and PowerPoint "Edit data" were checked with LibreOffice and the clipboard, never in real Office on Windows. | Low |
| 13 | **Dependency advisories.** | `bun audit`: 2 high (`image-size`, denial of service; reaches us through `pptxgenjs`, client-side only), 1 moderate (`minimatch`). | Low |
| 14 | **Three front ends.** | zohelo.com (its own UI and 101 open issues), zohelo-bi `main` (old portal) and the template. The 27 October plan assumed one design system shared by all three; the template has moved past it. | Medium |

## Does it follow the product principles?

| Principle | State |
|---|---|
| Business users first, not IT | Mostly. Dashboards ask questions; looks are grouped by question. The explorer still opens as a pivot tool with "wells", which is BI vocabulary. |
| Smart without AI | Yes. This is the strongest part. |
| Value in front, customisation behind | Partly. In the explorer the customisation is in front of the chart (finding 4). |
| Needs BI tools miss (Excel, PowerPoint, collections) | Built; not yet verified in Office. |
| Not another Power BI | At risk. 55 looks, 35 kinds and a 25-setting pane are the road there. |

## Recommendations, in order

1. **Stop widening; connect one real slice.** Take two domains (economy and labour, about 40 KPIs that zohelo-data can
   publish from Eurostat, GUS and NBP) through the whole path: published contract v2 → live provider → template.
   Everything not fed by real data in that slice stays visibly marked as a sample.
2. **Write published contract v2** from what the template actually needs: breakdown dimensions and members, EU and
   voivodeship geographies, release calendar, aggregation type per KPI (sum, average, ratio), polarity, and the
   measures the yardsticks use. It is the single most valuable document to write next.
3. **Put the chart first in the explorer.** One toolbar row (looks, export, customise), wells folded by default
   after the first chart, suggestions on demand. Then freeze the explorer's scope.
4. **Merge the template into `main` through a reviewed pull request,** delete the old portal and vega, close the
   issues that refer to the old UI, and protect `main`.
5. **Fix operations first thing:** the NBP build, the alert step, the six open alerts.
6. **Split the bundle by route** (home, dashboards, explorer, guide) and load the sample catalogue only in sample mode.
7. **Test with five real users** before building more: the owner's own contacts from consulting are the target
   audience. Watch them do three tasks (find a number, compare two regions, make a slide).
8. **Decide accounts** (Cloudflare Access identity is already there) before collections are promoted as a main feature.

# Roadmap after the template (2026-11-10)

Built on three documents of the same date: the audit (`docs/audit-2026-11-10.md`), the issue cross-check
(`docs/issues-crosscheck-2026-11-10.md`) and the analysis design (`docs/analysis-design.md`). It replaces the
product part of the 27 October programme plan; the data-platform part of that plan stands.

## Where we are, in one paragraph

The design template is a strong, well-tested prototype of the product the owner wants: business questions in
front, rules instead of AI, Excel and PowerPoint as first-class needs. It runs on 288 synthetic indicators. The
real catalogue publishes 7. The template has never been merged or reviewed, two of three repositories have an
unprotected `main`, the NBP pipeline has been failing since 9 November, and 896 open issues describe a plan the
template has overtaken. The next gain is not another feature: it is the first real number on the screen.

## The home page: proposal

The owner's question was what belongs on the home page. A business reader arrives with one of two needs: "what is
going on?" or "I need to do something". The current home answers neither first: it opens with personal
suggestions for a reader who has saved nothing yet, then eight unrelated figures.

The proposed layout ("Przegląd i drzwi", built as a third variant of the home layout):

| # | Section | What it answers | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask box (kept) | "I know what I am looking for" | existing hero |
| 2 | **Polska dziś** | "What is going on?" Five figures, each with its official yardstick, and four verdicts: euro criteria, economic balance, social targets, climate and innovation, each "met n of m" | the key figures and scorecards of "Polska w skrócie" |
| 3 | **Co nowego** | "What changed since my last visit?" Unusual moves, latest data, release calendar | moved from the Dashboards page |
| 4 | **Co chcesz zrobić?** | Four doors named by the task, not the tool: look up a number, compare and explore, understand what is going on, prepare a presentation; then the twelve topics | catalogue, explorer, analyses, collections, dashboards |
| 5 | Dla Ciebie | The reader's own things: second for a reader who has saved something, last on a first visit | existing |

Removed from the home page: the eight unrelated key figures, the "recently updated" list (replaced by "Co nowego")
and the catalogue statistics block.

If this layout is chosen, "Co nowego" leaves the Dashboards page, which then opens with "Polska w skrócie" and the
topic board. To see it: account panel → "Strona główna · układ" → "Przegląd i drzwi", or
`/?wariant=homeLayout:brief`.

## Stages

Each stage ends with something the owner can check without reading code.

### A. Stabilise (1–2 weeks)

| Work | Done when |
|---|---|
| Fix the NBP build, the alert step and the scheduler failures; close the three stale alerts | seven days of green scheduled runs |
| Merge the six open fixes on zohelo.com (wrong source credit, missing definitions) | the public site says nothing false |
| Protect `main` in zohelo and zohelo-bi (owner: two settings) | a direct push to `main` is refused |
| Merge the template into `main` through one reviewed pull request; delete the old portal and vega; split the bundle by route | `main` is the template; first load under 250 kB gzipped; checks green |
| Clean the backlog (decision 2) | about 100 open issues; none refers to the old portal |

### B. First real slice (4–6 weeks; the centre of the roadmap)

Goal: "Polska w skrócie", the economy dashboard and the labour dashboard show real numbers, with real verdicts.

| Work | Done when |
|---|---|
| **Published contract v2**, written from what the template needs: breakdown dimensions and members, EU and voivodeship geographies, release calendar, aggregation type per KPI (sum, average, ratio), polarity, the measures behind the yardsticks | one document both repositories test against |
| zohelo-data publishes about 40 KPIs for the slice (Eurostat, GUS, NBP): fix HICP (#2567), aggregation across sources (#2509), release calendar seed (#1097), the queued KPI publications (#2513–#2517) | the catalogue lists them as published, with contract tests green |
| Live provider complete: dimensions, geographies, next release, year-ago value | the three dashboards render with `VITE_DATA_MODE=live` and no sample marker |
| Everything outside the slice stays marked as sample data | no page mixes real and synthetic numbers without saying so |
| Five-user test with people from the owner's consulting contacts: find a number, compare two regions, make a slide | notes from five sessions and a short list of what to fix |

### C. Make it simple (2–3 weeks, alongside B)

| Work | Done when |
|---|---|
| Explorer: chart first. One toolbar row for looks, export and customise; wells folded after the first chart; suggestions on demand | the chart starts in the top third of a 1440×900 screen |
| Home page decided and the losing variants deleted | one home layout in the code |
| Excel paste and PowerPoint "Edit data" verified on Windows (owner, 15 minutes) | both confirmed or fixed |
| Explorer scope frozen: no new chart types or looks until the slice is live | written in the handover |

### D. Open (after B)

Legal pages and licence, the server-side security audit, accounts for favourites and collections, the public
address (decision 1), cookieless analytics. Done when a stranger can use the portal on real data and nothing on
it is sample.

### E. Grow (continuous)

More domains, one at a time, each through contract v2; the next analysis recipes (real terms, contributions to
change, what explains X); analyses and forecasts inside the explorer and in collections; "Co nowego" by e-mail;
the demo warehouse and portfolio last.

## Not now

New chart types and looks; a free dashboard editor; AI features; a public API; newsletter; the discovery briefs
(journal, research, products); the 170 further sources. Each is reasonable later and each would delay the first
real number.

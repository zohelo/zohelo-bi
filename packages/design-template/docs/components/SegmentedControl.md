A row of mutually exclusive options that switch a view: period, chart/table, language.

## Rules
- `role="group"` with a specific `aria-label` ("Widok wykresu inflacji", not "Widok"); each option a `button` with `aria-pressed`.
- Selected: `ink` fill, white text. 2–5 options, short labels, `white-space: nowrap`.
- Small variant (30px) inside chart cards; 44px on phone.

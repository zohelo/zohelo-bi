The container of every chart: finding as title, measure as subtitle, view toggle, actions, chart, legend, source.

## Consumer provides
Title (the finding), subtitle (measure, unit, frequency, range), the chart, the table data, source and dates.

## Rules
- Title `card-title`; subtitle `caption` `ink-3`.
- Wykres | Tabela segmented control and the ⋯ ActionMenu top-right.
- Legend under the chart for 2+ series; source line at the bottom with data date and next release.
- `surface`, 1px `line`, `radius-xl`, padding 18/20px, no shadow.
- Spans 12, 8, 6 or 4 columns; same height as its row neighbours.
- States: ChartStates.

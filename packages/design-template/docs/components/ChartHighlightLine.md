Linia z wyróżnieniem: jedna seria jest bohaterem, reszta to kontekst.

Group: Zmiana w czasie. Example: "Polska na tle sąsiadów" (HICP r/r, %, Polska i 3 kraje).

## When to use
Jedna seria jest bohaterem, reszta to kontekst.

## Avoid
Kilka równie jaskrawych kolorów; legenda daleko od linii.

## Colour and marks
Hero `series-1` 2.5px; others `series-context` 1.25px; direct labels at line ends; gridlines stop before the labels.

## Always
Title states the finding; subtitle states measure, unit, frequency and range; `role="img"` with a one-sentence `aria-label`; Wykres | Tabela toggle; source line; phone variant drawn for 340px with 11px text. Draw with `kit/zchart.js` as the visual reference; production charts come from `packages/visuals`.

Top of every page: the h1, one sentence stating the main finding, and the page's period control.

## Rules
- `page-title` (30/38 Inter Tight 700), 24/32 below 768px. One h1 per page.
- Lead sentence in `body-l` `ink-2`: a finding, not a description of the page ("Inflacja wróciła do pasma celu", not "Tu znajdziesz dane o inflacji").
- Period control: segmented control, right-aligned, wraps under the title on narrow screens; options are nouns of time (1 rok, 5 lat, 10 lat, Max).
